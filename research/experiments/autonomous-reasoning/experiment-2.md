# Experiment 2: Reflection-Based Reasoning

## Objective

Measure benefits of self-reflection and iterative critique loops.

---

## Hypothesis

Reflection cycles improve:

- Code quality
- Architecture robustness
- Test coverage
- Security posture

---

## Experiment Design

Pipeline:

Generate
→ Review
→ Reflect
→ Improve
→ Re-score

Iterations:

- 0 Reflection
- 1 Reflection
- 3 Reflections
- 5 Reflections

---

## Evaluation Metrics

- Defect density
- Security findings
- Maintainability score
- Execution success rate

---

## Risks

- Cost increase
- Latency increase
- Over-optimization

---

## Success Criteria

≥20% improvement in quality score
≤10% increase in cost
